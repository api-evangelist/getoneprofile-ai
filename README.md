Oneprofile provides a platform to find, research, and book your next customers. It offers tools for lead generation, outreach, and relationship management, helping businesses discover and engage prospects efficiently. The service integrates data enrichment, contact discovery, and automated outreach to streamline sales pipelines.

**Areas:** Sales Outreach · AI Research · Email Automation · LinkedIn Outreach · CRM Integration

## How this profile is made

API Evangelist profiles a company's public API surface the way a developer would find it: the website, developer portal and documentation, the specifications it publishes, its public repositories, and its pricing, status and changelog pages. From that it derives the properties above — contracts split per API, workflows, schemas, collections, governance rules, plans and rate limits — and scores them against the published [Kin Score rubric](https://github.com/api-evangelist/kin-score).

The artifacts are published on [APIs.io](https://apis.io), not in this repository. This repository is the public record for Oneprofile: the place to say something is wrong, missing or out of date, and to ask for a re-score or a removal.
